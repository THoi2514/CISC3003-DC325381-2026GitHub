Reflection and Future Directions

Studying Computer Science at the University of Macau has helped me grow from a beginner who only knew basic syntax to someone who can design and implement complete systems. Group projects taught me the importance of communication and version control (Git), while challenging courses pushed me to think more deeply about efficiency and scalability.

I have become more resilient when facing bugs and more confident in explaining technical concepts. However, I still need to improve my system design skills and gain more experience with large-scale projects.

After graduation, I plan to gain 2–3 years of industry experience as a software engineer (preferably in full-stack or AI-related roles), then consider pursuing a Master’s degree in Computer Science or Artificial Intelligence. Long-term goal is to contribute to smart city or sustainable tech solutions, especially in the Greater Bay Area.
